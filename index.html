Earth Jukebox

One flash is one click. Left on the globe is your left ear. You hear the side of the Earth that faces you. Nothing is turned into notes: the only control is how fast time runs. Slow, you hear single flashes. Faster, they merge into a hiss that swells each summer. Fastest, a whole year passes in a few thousandths of a second and the seasons themselves become the pitch.

The hand on the scope is the calendar. One turn is one year, January at the top. The line around the rim is how much lightning the visible side had through the year.
