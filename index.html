Earth Jukebox

Listen to NASA animations of a changing planet. The page reads each frame of the picture, region by region, and plays what it sees as notes in a musical scale.

Sound only mode. The picture is hidden; the sound and descriptions continue.

Player

Download WAV

How it sounds

The picture is cut into a grid. The player reads it one column at a time, left to right, and each column is one beat. A region with sea ice in it plays a note. Notes on the left come from the left speaker, notes on the right from the right. A low chord underneath gets quieter as the ice shrinks. A small bell rings at the start of each month.
